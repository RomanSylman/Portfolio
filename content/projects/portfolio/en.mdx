This site. The source link will appear only after the repository is on GitHub.

## The problem

The public page had to show interface and 3D work, not a list of technologies. The copy had to stay limited to facts from experience, with no invented metrics.

## My role

I built the site myself: architecture, scene, both languages, case studies and the quality checks.

## Key decisions

- Next.js 16 with the App Router. Ukrainian and English pages are statically generated with next-intl, and the locale is always in the URL.
- One WebGL context. The background and the card images are separate `drei` `View`s, not extra canvases.
- Particles morph between shapes on the GPU from the scroll stage. Scrolling is Lenis, tied to GSAP ScrollTrigger.
- Glow lives in the particle shader. Bloom postprocessing is not used: it fits poorly with the scissor rendering of `View`.
- The structure is Feature-Sliced Design, and Steiger checks the layer boundaries. Decisions are recorded as ADRs.
- Case studies are MDX read on the server with `@mdx-js/mdx`, without the RSC context of `@mdx-js/react`.

## The result

- The site builds statically for both languages and for the case study pages.
- CI runs the full check, the production build and Lighthouse CI with thresholds. I am not quoting measured scores here: a threshold in config is a requirement, not a report of a run.
